Small plates. No pretense.

Eatzers Dine started from a simple idea: dinner doesn't need three courses and a dress code to feel like an occasion. We built a small-plates menu so you can order two dishes or ten, share everything, and leave when you're actually full — not when the menu decided you should be.

The room is casual, cosy, and a little bit trendy — the kind of place regulars bring first dates and first-time visitors bring their whole itinerary. Come for a quick bite between meetings, or camp out with friends for the evening. Both are the point.

We seat solo diners at the counter, groups at the long table, and everyone in between wherever there's room.
